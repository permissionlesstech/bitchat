## Short version
Nearsend Chat has **no servers, no accounts and no analytics**. Messages travel directly between phones over Bluetooth.
We do not receive, store or sell any of your data.

## What the app stores on your phone
- Your nickname, a random device id and a private encryption key (iOS Keychain / app-private storage on Android).
- Your conversations, so they are still there after you restart the app.
- Which contacts you marked as verified.

"Erase everything" (You tab, or triple-tap the title) deletes all of it and creates a new identity. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## What other people nearby can see
- That your phone is running Nearsend Chat, your nickname, your random device id and your public key.
- **Direct messages** are end-to-end encrypted. Phones that relay them cannot read them, but can see who they are from/to and when.
- **Nearby channel** messages are public and unencrypted: everyone in range can read them.

## Permissions
- **Bluetooth** — to find nearby phones and exchange messages. Required.
- **Notifications** — optional; to alert you to new messages. 
- **Location (Android 11 and older only)** — Android requires this permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not read your location.

## Children, sharing, retention
We do not knowingly collect data from anyone. Because nothing is sent to us, we have nothing to share, retain or delete on our side.
